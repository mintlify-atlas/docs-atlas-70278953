## Models at a glance

| Model | Task | Size | Key Capability |
|-------|------|------|----------------|
| [VibeVoice-ASR](/models/vibevoice-asr) | Speech Recognition | 7B | 60-min single-pass, 50+ languages |
| [VibeVoice-TTS](/models/vibevoice-tts) | Text-to-Speech | 1.5B | 90-min multi-speaker generation |
| [VibeVoice-Realtime](/models/vibevoice-realtime) | Streaming TTS | 0.5B | ~200ms first-audio latency |

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install VibeVoice">
    Clone the repository and install the package. See the [Installation guide](/guides/installation).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your model">
    Pick the right model for your use case. Read the [Model Overview](/models/overview) to compare options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run inference">
    Follow the [Inference guide](/guides/inference) to generate speech or transcribe audio.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fine-tune (optional)">
    Adapt VibeVoice-ASR to your domain using the [LoRA fine-tuning guide](/guides/finetuning-asr).
  </Step>
</Steps>
